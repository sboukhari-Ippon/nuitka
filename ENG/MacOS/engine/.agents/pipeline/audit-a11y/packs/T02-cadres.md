# T02: Frames — RGAA criteria 2.1 to 2.2

Grid adapted from RGAA 4.1.2 (DINUM, accessibilite.numerique.gouv.fr, Open Licence 2.0). WCAG 2.1 equivalence and static testability indicated for each criterion.

## Pack method
Take an inventory of every `<iframe>` and `<frame>` in your files, including those generated by components (embedded video players, maps, payment widgets, third-party content). Only two questions: does the frame have a `title`? Does that `title` say what the frame contains?

## Criteria

### 2.1 — Every frame has a frame title — WCAG 4.1.2 (A) — static
- **Where:** `title` attribute on every `<iframe>`/`<frame>`; in JSX: `title` prop (often forgotten on YouTube/Maps/Stripe embeds).
- **NC if:** at least one frame without a `title`. Group the occurrences (one finding, all locations).
- **NA if** no frame in your files.

### 2.2 — Every frame title is relevant — WCAG 4.1.2 (A) — partial
- **Provable NC:** generic or technical `title`: `title="iframe"`, `title="frame"`, `title="embed"`, raw identifier, copied URL, identical `title` on frames with different content.
- **AVM otherwise:** relevance (does the title really announce the frame's content?) is confirmed by viewing the page; expected: "Introduction video", "Access map", "Payment module".
